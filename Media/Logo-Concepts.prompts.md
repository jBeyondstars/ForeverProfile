## Settings profile cards

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single minimal, polished logo icon for ForeverProfiles, a WoW addon for saving and switching graphics configurations. Concept: TWO OVERLAPPING SETTINGS PROFILE CARDS. Two compact rounded rectangular cards are offset diagonally, both clearly recognizable as separate saved configuration sheets. Each card uses only a couple of bold horizontal slider tracks with simple round knobs in DIFFERENT positions to indicate different settings. Use a smart overlap that leaves enough of the rear card visible to recognize its sliders. Strong compact silhouette and carefully balanced negative space; thick consistent strokes, simple flat geometric vector-like shapes, excellent clarity at 64px. Colors restricted to warm muted matte gold #D7B46B and opaque warm charcoal #191713 for card interiors, with actual transparency outside the logo. No gradients, texture, glow, bevel, photorealism, ornate fantasy detail, letters, FP monogram, text, labels, brand words, watermark, arrows, or presentation mockup. Square composition with the mark centered and comfortable padding. Deliver one isolated icon, not a sheet of alternatives. Make the icon visibly about stored graphics settings profiles, not documents with text.

## Graphics quality comparison

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single minimal, polished logo icon for ForeverProfiles, a WoW addon that switches between high-quality and performance graphics configurations. Concept: ONE LANDSCAPE DIVIDED INTO TWO LEVELS OF DETAIL. A compact landscape pictogram shows a mountain silhouette and a pine tree, split down the middle by a clean straight vertical seam. The overall scene stays aligned and recognizable across the seam. One half is visibly richer: a few clean mountain facets and a pine with a few distinct branches. The other half is visibly simplified: the matching mountain reduced to a plain silhouette and the matching pine reduced to a basic triangle. Keep both halves iconic, graphic, and clean rather than realistic. No tiny details or texture. A minimal square image-like outline can unify the scene, but no monitor stand, device mockup, circular emblem, shield, or extra symbols. Strong clear silhouette, thick clean geometry, excellent clarity at small sizes. Only warm muted matte gold #D7B46B and opaque warm charcoal #191713 where needed; actual transparent background outside the intentional logo and in negative-space openings. Flat vector-like finish, no gradients, lighting, shadows, bevels, glitter, 3D, letters, FP initials, words, watermark, or explanatory labels. Square centered composition with comfortable padding. Deliver one isolated icon, not multiple alternatives.
